# Undrift Ideas

**Undrift Ideas** ([@UndriftIdeas](https://www.youtube.com/@UndriftIdeas)) is a YouTube channel publishing short animated videos about stoic philosophy and staying on course in everyday life. Quotations come from public-domain translations.

## Undrift Uploader

*Undrift Uploader* is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube, schedules their publication time and reads their statistics, using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
